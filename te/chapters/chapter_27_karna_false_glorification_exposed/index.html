    CRIME #1: Karna Orders the Disrobing¶

    Sabha Parva, Chapter 61, Verse 36-37 (BORI Critical Edition)

    Sanskrit:

    दुःशासनस्त्वथो राजन् कर्णेन प्रचोदितः।
    द्रौपद्याः सर्ववस्त्राणि अमर्षवशमास्थितः॥
    बलाद्धर्तुमुपक्रान्तः समक्षं सर्वधन्विनाम्।

    Translation:

    "Then Duḥśāsana, INSTIGATED BY KARNA (karṇena pracoditaḥ), overcome by fury,
    Began to forcibly strip all the garments (sarva-vastrāṇi) of Draupadī,
    In the presence of all the warriors."

    Analysis:

    Sanskrit Term Meaning Significance
    प्रचोदितः (pracoditaḥ) Instigated, urged, commanded Karna GAVE THE ORDER
    सर्ववस्त्राणि (sarva-vastrāṇi) All garments Complete public nudity intended
    बलात् (balāt) By force Violent assault
    समक्षं सर्वधन्विनाम् In front of all warriors Public humiliation

    VERDICT: Karna didn't just "watch" — HE ORDERED THE DISROBING.
